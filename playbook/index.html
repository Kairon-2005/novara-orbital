Your Singapore education journey, guided

ℹ️ How to read this playbook

Each section lists what you can do, then how to do it step by step. Look for New tags on the newest features and the colored callouts for tips and gotchas.

Sign up & onboarding

Create your account, then a short onboarding captures your background and goals so the AI can personalize everything that follows.

How to get started

  1. Open the app and choose Sign up. Pick your role — Student or Parent — and set a display name and password.
  2. As a student, complete onboarding: nationality, current school & curriculum (IB / A-Level / AP / O-Level), target university & programme, target enrollment year, interests, and budget.
  3. You land on your Dashboard. From here the AI can generate your roadmap immediately.

Link a parent account

Parents get a read-only window into their child's journey — in Simplified Chinese. Linking uses a short invite code, so no email is required.

  1. In the student app, open your profile and copy your 6-character invite code.
  2. The parent signs up as a Parent and enters the code on the Join screen.
  3. The accounts are linked. The parent now sees roadmap, deadlines, finances, and progress for that student.
💡 No email needed

Family connection is the web dashboard plus WeChat-friendly sharing — Novara never sends email.

Dashboard

Your home base: what's next, how ready you are, and quick links into every tool.

  • Upcoming deadlines pulled from your calendar and roadmap.
  • Readiness snapshot from your latest portfolio assessment.
  • Recent achievements and quick actions (generate roadmap, add a target, log an achievement).

AI Roadmap

A personalized, multi-year academic plan — exams, competitions, applications, and milestones — generated from your profile and grounded in the knowledge base for NUS/NTU.

How to generate & adopt a roadmap

  1. On Roadmap, tap Generate. The AI drafts a year-by-year plan (it stays a preview until you accept it).
  2. Review the milestones. Adopt to save it; this archives any previous active roadmap.
  3. Tick milestones off as you complete them. Your freemium quota allows a first generation plus a yearly refresh.
ℹ️ Preview, then commit

AI output is always shown for review before it changes your data — nothing is saved until you accept it.

Calendar

One master calendar for exams, application deadlines, CCAs, fees, and personal events — colored by type and source.

  • Events come from the AI (roadmap & application plans), finance (fee due dates), and your own manual entries.
  • Built-in reminders at 30 / 7 / 3 / 1 days before.
  • Export to .ics to sync with Apple/Google Calendar.
💡 Auto-filled from application plans

The Application Assistant can push deadlines straight here — see Universities & Assistant.

Portfolio

Log achievements, earn XP, and get an honest, dimension-based assessment of how your profile stacks up against your target.

How to use it

  1. Add achievements — competitions, academics, CCAs, volunteering, awards — each worth XP that builds your gamified total.
  2. Run a Portfolio Assessment: the AI scores you across dimensions and explains strengths and gaps versus your target programme.
  3. Use the gaps to drive your roadmap and what to add next.

Documents

A secure home for transcripts, certificates, passports, and application files. Uploads are private to you and stored in encrypted storage.

  • Upload PDFs or images; the AI extracts the text and classifies each document so it can feed the assistant and assessment.
  • Grant a linked parent read access per document when you choose.
⚠️ Your files stay private

Documents are visible only to you unless you explicitly share a document with a linked parent.

Universities & Application Assistant New

Track every target school, get an AI gap analysis, and build a structured application plan — application window, deadlines, and a document checklist — then push the deadlines straight to your calendar.

Build a plan from the official page

Some university pages can't be scraped. So you hand Novara the page — by link, paste, or upload — and the assistant grounds the plan in that content (never invented dates).

  1. Open a target and choose 🔗 From official page.
  2. Paste a link (Novara fetches it), or paste the page text, or upload a PDF/screenshot. If a link can't be read, it asks you to paste — never a dead end.
  3. Review the generated plan. The assistant proposes calendar events, including a “submit all materials” milestone.
  4. Tick the events you want and Sync to calendar. Re-syncing never creates duplicates.
💡 Contribute to the wiki

When you build a plan from an official page, you can optionally submit that page for admin review so it improves the shared knowledge base — it's never shared until an admin approves it.

Knowledge Wiki

A curated, searchable knowledge base of official NUS/NTU admissions facts and pathway guides — the same corpus the AI cites when it answers you.

  • Browse docs grouped by university, pathway, and topic.
  • Search with hybrid retrieval (semantic + keyword) so exact scores and dates rank well.
  • Every doc shows its source URLs and a last-verified date.
ℹ️ Verified community cases feed in here

When an admission case is verified, its anonymized experience is added to the wiki so the AI can cite real outcomes.

School Navigator

A curated directory of Singapore schools — type, curriculum, zone, and details — to help you orient before you target.

  • Filter by school type and curriculum; see zone and key info at a glance.

Admission Case Library New

The heart of the community: real, verified admission outcomes — "what background got which offer" — plus the experience behind each result. Inspired by 一亩三分地 / Offer多多, adapted for Singapore admissions and anonymous by default.

Share your result (and get it verified)

  1. Tap + Share your result. Upload your offer letter / transcript — it auto-fills the form and privately backs verification.
  2. Fill in outcome (school, programme, route, result, year, scholarship), a compact background line, and your experience write-up.
  3. On publish, the AI cross-checks your proof against what you wrote. If they match you earn a ✅ Verified badge; if not, you'll see exactly which fields conflict.
⚠️ Proof stays private

Your uploaded documents are never shown to other users — only the verdict (a badge + which fields matched) is public. Only verified cases feed the wiki and count in statistics.

Browse the case library & positioning

  1. Open the 案例库 Cases tab and filter by school, programme, route, result, year, or “verified only”.
  2. Read the positioning panel: total verified cases, offer rate, result breakdown, and comparable backgrounds for your filter.
  3. Use 顶 / 踩 votes, 🔖 Save cases to 我的收藏, comment, and check 我的记录 for your own footprint.

Finance

Stay on top of the money side — tuition and homestay fees, insurance, and day-to-day spending.

  • Fee items with due dates flow into your calendar so nothing is missed.
  • Insurance policies with renewal dates and claims hotlines.
  • Expense logging by category for a monthly budget view. Visible to a linked parent in Chinese.

Homestay

Browse a directory of host families near Singapore schools, compare options, and shortlist.

  • Filter by zone, room type, monthly rate, and nearby schools.
  • Save listings to a shortlist and read reviews.

Parent Portal 简体中文

Once linked, parents get a read-only, Simplified-Chinese view of their child's journey — plus the communications tools families actually need.

  • 孩子概况 — dashboard, roadmap, deadlines, finances, and readiness, all in Chinese.
  • 沟通中心 — paste an English school notice; get a Chinese translation + summary, then an AI-drafted English reply from your Chinese intent.
  • Read-only by design — parents see progress without changing the student's data.

Admin Panel New

For operators (role: admin). The control room for everything that needs a human: moderation, verification oversight, the knowledge base, and directories. Reach it at /admin; non-admins are redirected.

ModuleWhat you do
DashboardCounts at a glance — users, cases by status, KB docs, pending moderation & contributions.
ModerationReview flagged cases & comments → Approve or Remove.
VerificationFilter cases by status; manually override the AI (force-verify / revoke / resolve) — which re-runs the wiki gate.
Knowledge baseList / refresh / delete KB docs; review user-contributed official pages → approve (ingest) or reject.
DirectoryManage schools and homestay listings without touching SQL.
UsersList users, view links/invite codes, and promote/demote admins.
ℹ️ Becoming an admin

The first admin is set once by a database update; after that, existing admins promote others from the Users module. Self-promotion is blocked at the database level.

FAQ

Is my data private?

Yes. Row-level security scopes data to its owner; documents and admission proofs are private to you; parents see only what linking grants, read-only.

What does “verified” mean on a case?

You uploaded a proof document and the AI confirmed it supports your claimed school and result. Only verified cases count in positioning stats and feed the wiki.

Does Novara email me?

No. Notifications are in-app; family connection is the web dashboard and WeChat-friendly sharing.

Why couldn't a university link be read automatically?

Some official pages block automated fetching. When that happens, paste the page text or upload a screenshot/PDF — the assistant works the same way.
