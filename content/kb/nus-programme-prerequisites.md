# NUS Programme Prerequisites (International Qualifications for Foreigners)

Subject prerequisites below are from the official NUS document "International Qualifications — Single Degree
Programme Subject Requirements" (accurate as of December 2025). They apply to applicants using foreign
qualifications (Gaokao, International A-Level, AP/American HS, and other international high school
qualifications). Prerequisites can change every year — always re-check the source.

## Computing programmes (School of Computing)

| Programme | Subject requirement | Interview/test |
|---|---|---|
| Common Computer Science Programmes | Year 12 or higher level pass in **Mathematics** | No |
| Business Analytics | Year 12 or higher level pass in Mathematics | No |
| Business Artificial Intelligence Systems | Year 12 or higher level pass in Mathematics | No |
| Information Security | Year 12 or higher level pass in Mathematics | No |
| Computer Engineering | Year 12 or higher level pass in Mathematics; students **without Year 12 Physics** must take specified Physics bridging courses | No |

## Business / Economics / Data Science

| Programme | Subject requirement | Interview/test |
|---|---|---|
| Business Administration | Year 12 or higher level pass in **Mathematics** | No |
| Data Science and Economics | **Strong** Year 12 pass or strong higher level pass in Mathematics | No |
| Humanities and Sciences (incl. Economics, Data Science & Analytics majors) | Refer to https://chs.nus.edu.sg/programmes/#prog-majors | No |
| Philosophy, Politics, and Economics | Refer to https://chs.nus.edu.sg/programmes/ppe | Yes |

## Engineering and Design (College of Design & Engineering)

| Programme | Subject requirement | Interview/test |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering | Year 12 or higher level pass in **Mathematics** | No |
| Architecture | Year 12 or higher level pass in Chemistry or Mathematics or Physics or Physical Science | Yes |
| Industrial Design | Year 12 or higher level pass in Arts or Economics or Mathematics or Physics | Yes |
| Landscape Architecture | Year 12 or higher level pass in Chemistry or Mathematics or Physics or Physical Science | Yes |

## Health sciences and Law (for completeness)

| Programme | Subject requirement | Interview/test |
|---|---|---|
| Medicine | Year 12+ pass in **Chemistry and (Biology or Physics)**; portfolio via NUS Medicine Admissions Portal; **UCAT required** | Yes |
| Dentistry | Year 12+ pass in **Chemistry and (Biology or Physics)** | Yes |
| Pharmacy / Pharmaceutical Science | Strong Year 12+ pass in **Chemistry and (Biology or Physics or Mathematics)** | Pharmacy: No |
| Nursing | Year 12+ pass in any two of: Biology, Chemistry, Computing, Physics, Physical Science, Mathematics/Further Mathematics | Yes |
| Law | High level of English proficiency | Yes |
| Food Science and Technology | Year 12+ pass in any two of: Chemistry, Biology, Physics, Physical Science, Computing, Mathematics/Further Mathematics | No |

## Programme-ranking rules (official footnotes)

- **Dentistry / Medicine**: must be ranked **1st or 2nd choice**; if 2nd, the 1st choice must be an
  interview programme. Only candidates with **actual results** submitted by the closing date are considered;
  shortlisted candidates attend tests/interviews in March/April.
- **Law**: must be ranked 1st, 2nd or 3rd choice; choices ranked above Law must be interview programmes.
- **Architecture, Industrial Design, Landscape Architecture, Nursing**: must be ranked 1st, 2nd or 3rd choice.

## Indicative Grade Profile (IGP) — competitiveness reference

The IGP is published only for **Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level** and **Polytechnic** applicants (IB and
other cohorts are too small). It is still the best official signal of programme competitiveness. Profiles
below are the 10th/90th percentile of applicants **offered places in AY2025/2026** (3 H2 + 1 H1 grades,
Grade C assumed for GP/PW). Meeting last year's profile does not guarantee admission.

| Programme | 10th percentile | 90th percentile |
|---|---|---|
| Common Computer Science Programmes | **AAA/A** | AAA/A |
| Business Analytics | AAA/A | AAA/A |
| Business Artificial Intelligence Systems | AAA/B | AAA/A |
| Information Security | AAA/B | AAA/A |
| Computer Engineering | AAA/A | AAA/A |
| **Business Administration** | AAA/C | AAA/A |
| **Engineering** | BBB/C | AAA/A |
| Data Science and Economics | AAA/A | AAA/A |
| Humanities and Sciences | ABB/C | AAA/A |
| Law / Medicine / Dentistry | AAA/A | AAA/A |
| Architecture | CCC/C | AAB/B |
| Industrial Design | BBC/B | AAA/A |
| Nursing | CCD/C | ABB/B |

Polytechnic GPA equivalents (10th–90th percentile): Common Computer Science 3.81–3.98; Computer
Engineering 3.81–3.99; Business Analytics 3.75–3.98; Information Security 3.88–3.98; Business
Administration 3.61–3.94; Engineering 3.57–3.94.

Notes:
- From AY2026/2027, the H1 content subject grade is no longer included in the University Admission Score
  (UAS) by default for Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level applicants; use the 3 H2 grades as the indicator.
- IGP takes into account Aptitude-Based Admissions (ABA) offers since AY2020/2021.
- Interpretation for international applicants: Computer Science, Business Analytics, Computer Engineering
  and Data Science & Economics are among the most competitive programmes (near-straight-A profiles);
  Engineering and Humanities & Sciences are relatively more accessible.
