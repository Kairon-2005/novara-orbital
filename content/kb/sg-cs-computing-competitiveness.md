# Computer Science Competitiveness: NUS vs NTU vs SMU

Compiled from AY2025/2026 official IGP data (via SGSchoolKaki's aggregation of NUS/NTU/SMU publications). Secondary source — cross-check the official IGP pages before relying on a specific cutoff.

## A-Level entry grades (3H2/1H1, AY2025/2026)

| University & course | 10th %ile | 90th %ile | Intake |
|---|---|---|---|
| NUS Computer Science | AAA/A | AAA/A | 979 |
| SMU Computer Science | AAB/A | AAA/A | 147 |
| NTU Computer Science | AAB/C | AAA/A | 527 |
| NUS Computer Engineering | AAA/A | AAA/A | 234 |
| NTU Computer Engineering | ABC/C | AAA/A | 229 |

Total ~1,653 CS places across the three universities; all heavily oversubscribed. NUS CS sits at the theoretical ceiling (AAA/A at both percentiles) — the same band as Medicine and Law.

CS cutoffs have risen faster than any other course in Singapore; if the trend holds, all three programmes may converge near AAA/A within a few years. Plan against next year's likely bar, not this year's published one.

## If grades fall short of CS: credible computing alternatives

- **NTU Computer Engineering (ABC/C; poly GPA ~3.66)** — same college (CCDS), significantly lower bar; many CE graduates work in pure software roles.
- **NTU Data Science & AI (CCDS)** — focused AI/statistics track; median starting salary ~S$6,250 (GES 2024).
- **NTU AI & Society (new, ~ABC/C)** — most accessible entry into CCDS; AI ethics/policy + technical foundations.
- **NUS Information Systems / Information Security** — School of Computing, lower cutoffs than NUS CS; InfoSec demand is strong.
- **SMU Computing & Law (ABB/A, 18 places)** — niche tech-law double degree below pure-CS cutoff.
- **SIT / SUTD computing** — applied/design-centric programmes with lower cutoffs and strong employment outcomes.

## Application notes (beyond grades)

- H2 Mathematics is the single most important subject; an A there carries the most weight. H2 Computing/Further Math help but are NOT prerequisites — NTU's first year assumes no programming background.
- A small coding portfolio (GitHub projects with real users), hackathons (Hack&Roll, BuildingBloCS) and competitions (NOI) strengthen borderline and aptitude-based applications.
- NUS runs a computing aptitude test for borderline applicants; NTU/SMU may interview ABA candidates — be ready to discuss one project in depth and your motivation.

## Career context (for programme choice, not admission)

2024 GES median starting salary for NTU CS ≈ S$6,000–6,600/month; big-tech offers S$8,000–10,000+. Caveat increasingly cited across the industry: AI tooling is shrinking entry-level generalist roles — specialisations (security, infrastructure, ML systems) and domain pairings (CS + finance/health/law) are more defensible than "generic full-stack". Treat salary tables as a tiebreaker between programmes, not the reason to choose CS.
