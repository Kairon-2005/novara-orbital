# NTU Undergraduate Admission Routes (International Qualifications)

NTU has **one intake per academic year** (term typically commences late July). Applicants apply under the **application group that matches their high school qualification** — students studying the Gaokao curriculum in China apply under the *PRC National College Entrance Exam (NCEE) / Gao Kao* group, even if they also took AP or SAT tests on their own or did not sit Gaokao.

Key general rules (AY2026/27 exercise):

- Final results must be available by **31 July** of the application year (by **31 March** for Medicine and Renaissance Engineering) to qualify for that year's intake.
- Application fee: **SGD 25** for international applicants (non-refundable, paid online by VISA/MasterCard or PayNow).
- Supporting documents are uploaded online per the Checklist within **3 days after the application closing date**; documents in Chinese do **not** need English translation.
- In addition to minimum entry requirements, applicants must satisfy the programme's **minimum subject requirements (MSR)** — see the MSR PDF (emsr_intnl.pdf) and `ntu-subject-requirements.md`.
- Personal statements / recommendation letters are NOT required (except LKCMedicine); extra-curricular records are optional and "will not enhance one's chance of admission" per NTU.

## 1. PRC Gaokao Route (高考)

**Application group:** PRC National College Entrance Exam (NCEE) / studying in Gao Kao curriculum.
**Application period (AY2026/27):** 15 Oct 2025 – 20 Jan 2026.

### Academic requirements
- **With Gaokao score:** at least **80% of the average total Gaokao score** (e.g. roughly 600/750). Submission of the **CSSD verification report** (chsi.com.cn) is compulsory.
- **Yet to sit Gaokao:** current Gao 3 students with good passes in **Gao 1 and Gao 2** results may apply (applicants taking Gaokao in June 2026).
- **AST (Aptitude Scholastic Test):** optional, but excellent results in at least 3 AST subjects (English, Maths + one more) enhance admission chances. AST with Economics as the 3rd subject ⇒ eligible for non-Engineering/non-Science programmes only. AST results without Gaokao or Gao 1/Gao 2 transcripts are not accepted.
- **Interview:** final admission decisions include an interview; only shortlisted candidates are invited (notification ~Apr 2026, interview outcome by end Jun 2026).

### Changes from AY2027 onwards (per NTU)
- NTU will **no longer use Gao 1 / Gao 2 results** to determine interview eligibility.
- All applicants must submit **actual Gaokao results within 3 days** of release to be considered for admission interviews.
- Admission interviews will be conducted in **July** (for those sitting the 2027 Gaokao).

### English language requirement (compulsory for Gaokao applicants)
One of:
- **IELTS**: overall 6.0, Writing 6.0, Verbal/Speaking 6.0
- **TOEFL iBT**: overall 90 with Speaking 25 (tests taken before Jan 2026); for the new-scale TOEFL iBT taken from Jan 2026 onwards, plan for minimum overall **5.0 with Speaking 5.0** (figure on NTU's general international-qualifications page; the Gaokao sub-page lists 4.5/4.5, but the same new scale applies to both, so the higher 5.0 is the safe baseline — and English requirements may be more stringent for highly competitive programmes)
- **SAT**: at least **1250** (redesigned SAT, Mar 2016 onwards; essay not required)
- **PTE Academic**: overall 55, Speaking 55
- **ACT with Writing**: composite at least **30** (one sitting)
- **AST English Test**: at least **215 marks**
- **C1 Advanced (CAE)**: overall 170, Writing 170, Speaking 170

Validity: IELTS/TOEFL/PTE scores must be within **2 years** of the application deadline; SAT/ACT within **5 years**. NTU notes English requirements may be **more stringent** for highly competitive, non-Science/non-Engineering, double degree and double major programmes.

### Other notes
- NTU Entrance Examination: **not required** for Gaokao applicants.
- International Science Olympiad medallists (IMO, IOI, IPhO, IChO, IBO, IOAI etc.) are considered **more favourably** for related programmes ("Direct Admission" consideration).
- Eligible for all programmes if MSR met; results after 1 April ⇒ not eligible for LKCMedicine / Renaissance Engineering that year.

## 2. International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Route

**Application period (AY2026/27):** 15 Oct 2025 – 19 Mar 2026. Outcome: end Apr – end Jul 2026.

- **Minimum requirement:** "A very good combination of 5s, 6s and 7s" across 3 Higher Level and 3 Standard Level subjects, plus good EE and TOK grades.
- Both **Math: Analysis and Approaches (MAA)** and **Math: Applications and Interpretation (MAI)** are acceptable where Mathematics is a prerequisite; no preference between them.
- Applicants must authorise **IBO to release results directly to NTU** for verification.
- Applicants awaiting May exam results: key in actual results and upload result slip within **3 days** of release; **no provisional offers** on predicted grades. Outcome within 1–2 weeks after actual results submitted.
- May-exam candidates are not eligible for LKCMedicine, Renaissance Engineering, Arts/Science (Education) in that exercise.
- English test (IELTS/TOEFL etc.) generally **not required** for IB students unless English was not the medium of instruction / taken only as a second language.

## 3. International (UK) A-Level / Cambridge Pre-U Route

**Application period (AY2026/27):** 15 Oct 2025 – 19 Mar 2026. Outcome: May – end Jun 2026.

- **Minimum requirement:** good passes in at least **4 Advanced Level subjects taken in the same sitting** (Cambridge/CAIE, Edexcel, OCR, AQA, LRN; same board or mixed). A-Level subjects with final papers in different sittings are unacceptable.
- Applicants with only **3 A-Level subjects** in one sitting may still apply (case-by-case), but this concession does **not** apply to international students applying on subsidised (MOE Tuition Grant) fee basis.
- **Cambridge Pre-U:** good passes in all subjects of the Pre-U Diploma in one sitting, or a combination of A-Level + Pre-U subjects (4 total, single sitting).
- Applicants sitting May/Jun 2026 A-Level exams (results in August) are **not considered** in the AY2026/27 exercise — apply the following year.
- English test generally not required if English was the medium of instruction.

## 4. American High School Diploma / AP Route

**Application period (AY2026/27):** 15 Oct 2025 – 19 Mar 2026. Outcome: mid Jun – mid Jul 2026.

- **Minimum requirement:** good CGPA/marks in the High School Diploma **AND** scores of **4 and 5 in 3 or more AP exams** (College Board).
- **AP Calculus BC is compulsory** for Applied Computing in Finance, Engineering, and most Science programmes. **AP Physics C** is required where Physics is an MSR prerequisite. Choose AP subjects per the programme's MSR. NTU notes most international applicants apply with **more than 4 APs**.
- AP scores must reach NTU by **15 July 2026**; later scores are not considered.
- Final-year students must provide at least up to **Grade 12 Semester 1** results at application; final transcript within 3 days when available.
- **English requirement:** only if English was not the medium of instruction or was taken as a second language — IELTS 6.0 (W6.0/S6.0) or TOEFL iBT 90 (Verbal 25) or SAT 1250 or PTE Academic 55 (Speaking 55) or ACT with Writing 30 or C1 Advanced 170.
- NTU score-reporting codes: **SAT 3721, ACT 1873, TOEFL DI 0138**.
