# NTU Undergraduate Programmes (Key Colleges)

NTU's main colleges: College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS), College of Business (Nanyang Business School, NBS), College of Engineering (CoE), College of Science, College of Humanities Arts and Social Sciences, Lee Kong Chian School of Medicine, NTU Honours College, NIE. Full searchable list: https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate-programmes

## College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS)

3,000+ undergraduates; **16 undergraduate programmes**. Rankings cited by NTU: #1 Singapore Computer Science (CSRankings 2026), #1 World AI (Shanghai Rankings 2025), #4 World Data Science & AI (QS 2026).

### Single degrees (all 4-year direct honours)
| Programme | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| BComp (Hons) in Computer Science | Core CS: algorithms, data structures, software systems |
| BComp (Hons) in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI) | Jointly offered by CCDS & SPMS |
| BComp (Hons) in Artificial Intelligence and Society | Technical AI + ethical AI design/deployment |
| BEng (Hons) in Computer Engineering | Hardware-software co-design, embedded systems |

### Double degrees
- Computer Science and Business (specialisation in Business Analytics) — with NBS, 4 years
- Computer Engineering and Business (specialisation in Business Analytics) — with NBS, 4 years
- Computer Science and Economics
- Computer Engineering and Economics
- Accountancy & Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (ACDA) — with NBS

### Joint/interdisciplinary degrees
- Bachelor of Applied Computing in Finance (with NBS; note: AP Calculus BC compulsory for AP-route applicants)
- BSc in Economics and Data Science
- BSc in Mathematical and Computer Sciences (double major, with SPMS)

### Second majors available with CS / CE / DSAI
Business, Business (International Trading), Entrepreneurship, Sustainability, Data Analytics (with Computer Engineering).

### Premier track
- **Turing AI Scholars Programme (TAISP)** — premier undergraduate AI track with faculty mentorship and research-led inquiry.

## College of Engineering (CoE)

Five schools: CCEB (Chemistry, Chemical Engineering & Biotechnology), CEE (Civil & Environmental), EEE (Electrical & Electronic), MAE (Mechanical & Aerospace), MSE (Materials Science & Engineering). All BEng programmes are 4-year direct honours.

### Flagship BEng programmes
| School | Programmes |
| --- | --- |
| CCEB | Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering; Bioengineering; BEng Science with Double Major in Process Engineering & Synthetic Chemistry |
| CEE | Civil Engineering; Environmental Engineering; BSc Maritime Studies (also SkillsFuture Work-Study variants) |
| EEE | Electrical & Electronic Engineering; Information Engineering and Media (IEM); EEE (Part-Time) |
| MAE | Aerospace Engineering; Mechanical Engineering; **Robotics** (newer programme); Mechanical (Part-Time) |
| MSE | Materials Engineering |

### Common add-ons across engineering
Second majors: Business, Business (International Trading), Data Analytics, Sustainability, Entrepreneurship, Society & Urban Systems, Future Foods (selected), Pharmaceutical Engineering / Medical Biology (MSE). Double degrees: BEng + BSocSc (Economics) for most schools.

### Premier engineering programmes
- **Renaissance Engineering Programme (REP)** — premier scholar programme; BEng Science + MSc in Technology Management in ~4.5 years; strong global component; final results must be available by 31 March of application year.
- **CN Yang Scholars Programme (CNYSP)** — NTU Signature Programme for future science/engineering leaders.
- **NTU-University Scholars Programme (NTU-USP)** — interdisciplinary scholars programme.
- NTU–SJTU (Shanghai Jiao Tong University) Dual Degree Programme (EEE).

## Notes for applicants

- Admission is to a specific programme (not a general college); each programme has minimum subject requirements (see `ntu-subject-requirements.md`).

- Highly competitive programmes for Chinese applicants typically include Computer Science, DSAI, double degrees with Business, and REP.
