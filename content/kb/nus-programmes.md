# NUS Undergraduate Programmes

NUS offers **60 majors, 54 second majors, and over 80 minors** across its colleges, faculties and schools.
Below is the official programme list from the NUS Office of Admissions, with intake numbers (places taken
up in AY2025/2026) where published on the Indicative Grade Profile page.

## School of Computing (SoC)

| Programme | AY2025/2026 intake |
|---|---|
| Common Computer Science Programmes (Computer Science and related) | 893 |
| Business Analytics | 274 |
| Business Artificial Intelligence Systems (formerly "Information Systems") | 93 |
| Information Security | 47 |
| Computer Engineering (joint with College of Design & Engineering) | 203 |

Notes:
- "Common Computer Science Programmes" is the admission gateway to NUS Computer Science.
- **Business Artificial Intelligence Systems (BAIS)** is the renamed Information Systems programme — the
  closest named "AI" undergraduate admission programme listed by the Office of Admissions. NUS does not list
  a separate standalone "Artificial Intelligence" admission programme on the OAM programme page; AI-related
  study is offered within Computing programmes (see source).
- Computer Engineering is jointly offered by SoC and the College of Design & Engineering (CDE).

## NUS College and special programmes

- **NUS College (NUSC)**: the Honours College of NUS; students take an enriched interdisciplinary
  curriculum alongside their primary degree and graduate with their NUS degree plus an NUSC certificate.
- **Double Degree Programmes (DDP)**: two degrees, shortened via double-counting; admission at entry or
  after 3rd/4th semester.
- **Concurrent Degree Programmes (CDP)**: Bachelor's + Master's in ~4.5–5 years.
- **Joint Degree Programmes**: single jointly-awarded degree with an overseas partner university.
- **Double Major / Minor programmes**: at entry or up to end of 5th semester.
- Special tracks: Engineering Scholars Programme (E-Scholars), NUS Engineering and Medicine Track
  (with Duke-NUS), Innovation and Design Programme (iDP), Special Programme in Science (SPS),
  Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme (UROP).

## Notes on intake figures

- Intake numbers are from IGP Table 3 ("programme places taken up in AY2025/2026"); double degree
  programmes are excluded, and numbers change year to year.
- Indicative competitiveness for each programme is in nus-programme-prerequisites (IGP grade profiles).
