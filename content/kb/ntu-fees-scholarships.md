# NTU Tuition Fees, MOE Tuition Grant & Scholarships

## Annual tuition fees — programme offers accepted in 2026

The annual tuition fee is **fixed at the 2026 rate for the whole degree duration**. "Subsidised" = receiving the MOE Tuition Grant. Amounts for international students (IS) and non-subsidised students are inclusive of 9% GST; SC/SPR figures exclude GST (GST subsidised by MOE).

| Programme group | SG Citizens | SG PRs | ASEAN IS (subsidised) | All other IS (subsidised) | Non-subsidised (no grant) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All programmes (except below) | S$8,300 | S$11,600 | S$18,050 | **S$21,400** | S$40,600 (lab-based) / S$36,350 (non-lab) |
| Accountancy / Business group* | S$9,500 | S$13,300 | S$20,700 | **S$21,800** | S$45,600 |
| Medicine (MBBS) | S$36,100 | S$51,850 | S$80,850 | S$91,250 | Not applicable |
| Renaissance Engineering Programme | S$13,500 | S$20,100 | S$31,000 | S$32,000 | Not applicable |

\* Accountancy; Accountancy (Sustainability Management and Analytics); Business; Accountancy & Business; Business & Computing; Business & Computer Engineering.

Practical reading for Chinese (non-ASEAN) international students: **~S$21,400–21,800/year with MOE Tuition Grant**, or **~S$36,350–45,600/year without** (CS/Engineering are lab-based: S$40,600).

Other costs: application fee S$25; miscellaneous fees (see NTU Miscellaneous Fees page); estimated living costs S$970–1,725/month (see `ntu-admissions-routes.md`).

## MOE Tuition Grant (TG)

- Provided by Singapore's Ministry of Education; covers a **substantial portion of full tuition fees** — recipients pay only the subsidised fee above.
- Eligibility: full-time undergraduates admitted on a subsidised-fee basis, up to the normal programme duration (3–4 years). Transfer/re-admitted students get TG for the normal duration minus semesters already used.
- **Service obligation:** all non-Singaporean recipients (SPRs and international students) must sign a Tuition Grant Agreement and are **contractually obliged to work in a Singapore-registered entity for 3 years after graduation**.
- **Two sureties** (any nationality, aged 21–under 65, not undischarged bankrupts, not TG recipients with outstanding obligations) are required for the agreement.
- How to apply: international students **opt in for TG in the online admission application form**; if offered, decide to accept with/without TG by the stated deadline (18 July for the 2025 intake). Registration is done after matriculation on MOE's TG portal using Singpass (requires Student's Pass + Singpass; registration starts ~September; for AY2025/26 freshmen the online registration window was 2–6 Feb 2026 with online signing 7–13 Feb 2026).
- Students who do not apply for TG or do not complete registration/signing pay **non-subsidised fees**.
- MOE TG info: https://go.gov.sg/tgwebsite

## Scholarships overview

NTU offers **100+ scholarships**, grouped into three categories (per NTU's scholarships page):

1. **Merit-based** — recognising academic excellence. Flagship examples featured by NTU:
   - **Nanyang Scholarship / Nanyang Global Scholarship** — NTU's premier merit scholarship.
   - **Renaissance Engineering Programme (REP) Scholarship** (note: REP itself is restricted to SC/SPR per the MSR).
   - **CN Yang Scholarship** (science & engineering research track).
   - **NTU-University Scholars Programme (NTU-USP) Scholarship**.
   - **NBS Global Leaders Programme Scholarship** (business).
   - **Turing AI Scholars Programme** (CCDS, AI).
2. **Need-based** — alleviating financial barriers (bursaries, financial aid; see NTU Financial Aid page).
3. **Talent-based** — outstanding non-academic achievement (e.g. **NTU Talent Scholarship** for sports/arts).

### Application notes for international applicants
- Scholarship applications are submitted **online within the admission application period** via the application "Check Status" portal — there is no separate later window for freshmen.
- For some qualification groups (e.g. Malaysia STPM/UEC), an English test (IELTS/TOEFL/SAT/etc.) is compulsory **when applying for scholarships**; Gaokao applicants already must submit an English test.
- Scholarship details/eligibility per scheme: https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/scholarships/freshmen
- Scholarships generally do not remove the MOE TG service obligation if the student takes the TG; specific terms vary by scheme — check the individual scholarship's terms.

## Caveats

- Fee figures apply to students **accepting offers in 2026**; each cohort's rates are published separately and locked for the programme duration.
- TG registration dates and deadlines change each intake; the dates above are from the AY2025/26 cycle as shown on the NTU page at verification time.
- Scholarship quanta (stipend amounts, coverage) are not listed on the overview page — see individual scholarship pages before quoting amounts.
