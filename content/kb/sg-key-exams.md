# Key Exams: AEIS, PSLE, O-Level, A-Level, IB DP

Each exam below is described by: what it is, who administers it, when it is held, and its role in NUS/NTU admission. National exam dates change yearly — verify on moe.gov.sg/national-exams-dates and seab.gov.sg.

## 1. AEIS (Admissions Exercise for International Students)

- **What:** MOE's centralised admission test for international students seeking places in mainstream MOE schools at **Primary 2–5 and Secondary 1–3** (plus the supplementary S-AEIS for P2–4 / Sec 1–2).
- **Administers:** Singapore **Ministry of Education (MOE)**.
- **When:** applications open ~**July**; tests held ~**September–October**; successful candidates start school the following **January**. S-AEIS runs early in the year for same-year entry. (Official: moe.gov.sg, verified Jun 2026; 2026 cycle dates tentative.)
- **Content:** English and Mathematics at the level applied for. Test format and any English-qualification requirements are updated by MOE periodically — always check the current "Test details" page on moe.gov.sg before advising.
- **Role in university admission:** none directly — it is purely a school-entry gate. Its importance is that it is the **only** door into MOE mainstream schools (and hence the O-Level → JC → A-Level route) for international students. Passing is not guaranteed and placement depends on vacancies.

## 2. PSLE (Primary School Leaving Examination) — brief

- **What:** national exam at the end of **Primary 6** (age ~12), scored with Achievement Levels (AL1–AL8 per subject).
- **Administers:** **Singapore Examinations and Assessment Board (SEAB)**, a statutory board of MOE.
- **When:** written papers around **late September–early October**; results in November.
- **Role:** determines secondary-school posting (including Integrated Programme places). Most international students arriving for secondary school bypass PSLE via AEIS, so it matters mainly for students who enter at primary level.

## 3. Singapore-Cambridge GCE O-Level

- **What:** national exam at the end of secondary school (typically **Secondary 4 Express, age ~16**); grades A1–F9, aggregate computed as L1R5/ELR2B2. (Note: MOE is transitioning national secondary certification to the Singapore-Cambridge Secondary Education Certificate under Full Subject-Based Banding; treat O-Level as the reference for current advice and verify the candidate's cohort.)
- **Administers:** jointly by **MOE, SEAB and Cambridge** (Cambridge University Press & Assessment / UCLES) — hence "Singapore-Cambridge".
- **When:** exams spread across the year (mother tongue/orals mid-year), with the **main written window ~mid-October to mid-November**; results the following **January** (2026 written papers: 19 Oct–10 Nov; results ~mid-Jan 2027, per SEAB/MOE).
- **Role in university admission:** indirect but pivotal — O-Level results drive **JAE posting** to JC (A-Level route) or Polytechnic. NUS/NTU also look at O-Level English/subject grades as supporting criteria for some poly-route and prerequisite checks. Private candidates (including international students outside MOE schools) can register through SEAB.

## 4. Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level

- **What:** the pre-university qualification taken at the end of **JC2** (age ~18) or Millennia Institute Year 3. Curriculum: typically 3 H2 + 1 H1 content subjects plus General Paper/Knowledge & Inquiry, Project Work and Mother Tongue; grades A–E; University Admission Score commonly computed out of 90 (rank points).
- **Administers:** jointly by **MOE, SEAB and Cambridge**.
- **When:** mid-year orals/early papers from ~June; **main written window ~late October–late November**; results the following **February** (2026: main papers 2–27 Nov; results ~late Feb 2027, per SEAB/MOE).
- **Role in university admission:** the **primary qualification of the largest NUS/NTU applicant group** ("Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level"). Applications open after results (~Feb) and close ~March; outcomes by ~May; matriculation in August. Competitiveness per programme is published via NUS/NTU **Indicative Grade Profiles**. Note: the *Singapore-Cambridge* A-Level is a distinct, generally harder exam than Cambridge *International* A-Levels offered by private schools (the latter falls under the international-qualifications application group).

## 5. IB Diploma Programme (IB DP)

- **What:** 2-year pre-university diploma: 6 subjects (3 HL + 3 SL) plus Theory of Knowledge, Extended Essay and CAS; scored out of **45**.
- **Administers:** the **International Baccalaureate Organization (IBO)**, Geneva — not SEAB.
- **When:** two exam sessions — **November** (used by most Singapore-based IB schools such as ACS(I) and SJI; results early January) and **May** (most international schools such as UWCSEA; results early July).
- **Role in university admission:** feeds the dedicated **IB Diploma** application group at NUS/NTU, treated on par with A-Level applicants. November-session candidates fit the standard cycle (outcomes ~May); May-session candidates apply with predicted grades and receive NUS outcomes by ~third week of July for August matriculation (official: NUS OAM).

## 6. Quick comparison

| Exam | Body | Typical age | Main sitting | University role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AEIS | MOE | 8–15 | Sep–Oct | School entry only |
| PSLE | SEAB | 12 | Sep–Oct | Secondary posting |
| O-Level | MOE/SEAB/Cambridge | 16 | Oct–Nov | JC/Poly posting |
| SC A-Level | MOE/SEAB/Cambridge | 18 | Oct–Nov | Main NUS/NTU group |
| IB DP | IBO | 18 | May or Nov | NUS/NTU IB group |

**Guidance:** for a Chinese student planning backwards from NUS/NTU, the decisive exams are the A-Level or IB DP (or poly GPA); AEIS and O-Level are gates that determine *which* of those final qualifications is reachable.
