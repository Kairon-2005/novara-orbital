# NTU Minimum Subject Requirements — International Qualifications (AY2026/27)

Source: NTU "Minimum Subject Requirements for Students with International Qualifications" PDF (information correct as at February 2026). Applicants must have completed at least 12 years of general education or be taking high school national exams in the application year. "Senior High School Level" ≈ Gaokao subjects / A-Level / AP / IB HL-SL; "Junior High School Level" ≈ Zhongkao / IGCSE-O Level.

## College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS)

| Programme | Minimum subject requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Computer Science (BComp) | Mathematics at Senior HS Level, OR Physics at Senior HS Level (UK CIE A-Level Computer Science acceptable in lieu of Physics) |
| Computer Science with Second Major (Business / Entrepreneurship / Sustainability) | Same as Computer Science |
| Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (incl. with 2nd Major in Sustainability) | Mathematics at Senior HS Level |
| Artificial Intelligence and Society | Mathematics at Senior HS Level, OR Physics at Senior HS Level (CIE A-Level CS acceptable) |
| Computer Engineering (BEng, incl. second majors) | Mathematics at Senior HS Level, AND Physics/Chemistry/Biology at Senior HS Level, AND Physics at Junior HS Level (only if Physics not read at Senior HS Level) |
| Business and Computing (Double Degree) | Mathematics OR Physics at Senior HS Level (CIE A-Level CS acceptable), AND a good grade in English at Senior HS Level |
| Business and Computer Engineering (Double Degree) | Mathematics at Senior HS Level, AND Physics/Chemistry/Biology at Senior HS Level, AND Physics at Junior HS Level, AND a good grade in English at Senior HS Level |

Note: "Mathematics at Senior HS Level" applies only to applicants who have not read Additional Mathematics at Junior HS Level.

## College of Engineering (CoE)

Standard pattern for most BEng programmes — **Mathematics at Senior HS Level AND Physics/Chemistry/Biology at Senior HS Level AND Physics at Junior HS Level** (Junior-level Physics needed only if Physics not read at Senior HS Level):

- Aerospace Engineering (+ second majors)
- Civil Engineering (+ second majors)
- Electrical and Electronic Engineering (+ second majors)
- Environmental Engineering (+ second majors)
- Information Engineering and Media (+ second majors)
- Mechanical Engineering (+ second majors)
- Robotics (+ second majors)

Variations:

| Programme | Minimum subject requirements |
| --- | --- |
| Bioengineering; Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering; Materials Engineering (and their common second majors) | Mathematics at Senior HS Level AND Physics/Chemistry/Biology at Senior HS Level (no Junior HS Physics requirement); the Pharmaceutical Engineering / Medical Biology second-major variants additionally require Physics at Junior HS Level |
| Process Engineering and Synthetic Chemistry (BEng Science double major, NEW) | Mathematics at Senior HS Level AND Chemistry at Senior HS Level; selection test/interview: Yes |
| Maritime Studies (BSc, incl. 2nd Major Business/Sustainability) | Additional Mathematics at Junior HS Level |
| Maritime Studies with 2nd Major in Data Analytics | Mathematics at Senior HS Level |

Many engineering programmes are also offered as double degree with Economics, or with Minor in Business / International Trading.

## Renaissance Engineering Programme (REP)

- Mathematics at Senior HS Level, AND Physics/Chemistry/Biology at Senior HS Level, AND Physics at Junior HS Level, AND a good grade in General Paper/English at Senior HS Level. Selection test/interview: **Yes**.
- **REP is only open to Singaporeans and Singapore PRs** (per the MSR footnote). Leads to BEng Science + MSc in Technology Management.

## Selected other programmes (context)

- **Medicine (LKCMedicine):** Chemistry at Senior HS Level AND Biology/Physics at Senior HS Level; plus UCAT (taken 7 Jul – 26 Sep 2025 for AY2026/27), personal statement, two referee reports; interview: Yes.
- **Economics and Data Science (BSc):** Mathematics at Senior HS Level AND good English at Senior HS Level.
- **Mathematical Sciences / Mathematical and Computer Sciences:** Mathematics at Senior HS Level (M&CS also requires Physics/Chemistry/Biology at Senior HS Level).
- **Chinese:** good grade in Chinese at GCE O-Level / **HSK Level 6**; interview: Yes.
- **Art, Design & Media:** Maths at Junior HS Level + good English at Senior HS Level + portfolio, personal statement/writing samples, creative project, observational drawing.

## Qualification-specific application notes (from same PDF)

- **PRC Gaokao:** Senior Middle 3 students sitting 2026 Gaokao apply with Senior Middle 1 & 2 results + English test (IELTS/TOEFL/SAT/ACT with Writing/PTE/C1 Advanced/AST English). Those who took Gaokao apply with Gaokao results + English test. AST scores should also be submitted if taken. Interview for selected candidates. Window: 15 Oct 2025 – 20 Jan 2026.
- **American HS Diploma:** apply with Grade 11 + Grade 12 Sem 1 results; **at least 3 AP exams compulsory**; AP Calculus BC compulsory for Engineering and most Science programmes; AP Physics C required where Physics is an MSR. AP scores by 15 Jul 2026. Window: 15 Oct 2025 – 19 Mar 2026.
- **A-Level (Cambridge/Edexcel/AQA/OCR, Brunei, HK, etc.):** at least **4 A-Level subjects in a single sitting**; 3 subjects in a single sitting may be considered for admission on **non-subsidised tuition fees** only. A-Level Mathematics or Further Mathematics required for Engineering and most Science programmes. Results available in August ⇒ apply next AY. Window: 15 Oct 2025 – 19 Mar 2026.
- **IB Diploma:** admission based on actual IB results (Nov-exam results by March closing date; May-exam results within 3 days of release); must authorise IBO to release results to NTU. Window: 15 Oct 2025 – 19 Mar 2026.
- Applicants whose final results are released after April 2026 are ineligible for Medicine, REP and Education programmes; after 15 Jul 2026 ⇒ ineligible for ADM.

## How to read MSR footnotes

- "+": Junior HS Physics applies only if Physics was not read at Senior HS Level.
- "*": also offered as double degree with Economics; "^": Minor in Business; "£": Minor in International Trading.
- For Gaokao applicants, Senior HS subjects map to Gaokao/Gao 3 curriculum subjects; Junior HS maps to Zhongkao-level subjects.
