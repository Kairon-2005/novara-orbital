# NUS Important Dates — AY2026/2027 Admissions Exercise

NUS notes that details are subject to change and applicants should check the Important Dates page regularly.
Status flags ([Closed]) reflect the page as of June 2026.

## Application periods by qualification

| Qualification | Application period (AY2026/2027) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **International Qualifications (except IB Diploma)** — includes Gaokao, International A-Level, American High School Diploma/AP, and other foreign qualifications | **3 December 2025 to 23 February 2026** | Closed |
| IB Diploma | 17 December 2025 to **23 February 2026** | Closed |
| Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level | **26 February 2026 (10am SGT) to 19 March 2026** | Closed |
| Polytechnic Diploma from Singapore | 17 December 2025 to 4 February 2026 | Closed |
| NUS High School Diploma | 17 December 2025 to 2 January 2026 | Closed |

For Chinese international students, the operative window is the **International Qualifications** one
(3 Dec – 23 Feb) — or the IB window if applying with the IB Diploma.

## Admissions outcomes

- **International qualifications (except IB)**: most applicants receive the outcome online **by July**.
- **Local qualifications / IB (Nov session or earlier)**: most applicants receive outcomes by the
  **3rd week of May**.
- **IB May 2026 examination takers**: outcomes by the **3rd week of July 2026**.
- Notification is sent to the email address in the application form; the electronic admission outcome
  letter is downloaded from the Applicant Portal.

## Updating final results (international qualifications)

Applicants sitting the following exams in 2026 must update and submit final results **within 3 days of
results release** through the Applicant Portal:

- Advanced Placement (AP) Test
- **Gaokao**
- HKDSE
- Indian Standard 12 (CBSE and ISC boards)
- STPM

Multiple/duplicate document submissions can affect processing and outcome.

## Accepting an offer

- **International qualifications**: accept through the **Applicant Portal** by the deadline stated on the
  electronic admission outcome letter.
- **Local qualifications and IB**: accept through the **Joint Acceptance Portal (JAP)**
  (https://www.jointacceptance.edu.sg/):
  - Window 1: 2 January – 25 May 2026
  - Window 2: 1 June – 16 June 2026
  - Acceptance is reflected on the Applicant Portal three calendar days after the acceptance deadline.
- NUS does not issue an acceptance status letter; status is viewable in the Applicant Portal.

## IB-specific deadlines (May 2026 session)

- Update IB Personal Code via Applicant Portal by **17 June 2026**.
- NUS must receive the IB transcript from the IB by **7 July 2026**.
- Dentistry/Medicine applicants: required documents and test scores (predicted IB + UCAT or SAT/ACT+AP)
  due by **23 February 2026**.

## Transfer applicants (for reference)

- AY2026/2027 Semester 1 transfer window: 3 February 2026 (12pm SGT) to 15 February 2026 (Closed);
  outcomes by June. Overseas-university undergraduates may apply as transfer applicants only if they are
  Singapore Citizens / PRs; current undergraduates of Singapore Autonomous Universities (NUS, NTU, SIT,
  SMU, SUSS, SUTD) may also apply.
