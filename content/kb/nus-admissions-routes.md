# NUS Undergraduate Admissions Routes (AY2026/2027 cycle)

NUS admits international students (foreigners) through several qualification routes. The four most relevant
for Chinese international students are: **Gaokao**, **International A-Level**, **American High School
Diploma + AP/SAT/ACT**, and **IB Diploma**. Admission is holistic: academic merit, non-academic
achievements, interviews/tests where required, and open competition among all eligible applicants.
Application closing date for international qualifications in the AY2026/2027 cycle was **23 February 2026**.

## Route 1: Gaokao (PRC National College Entrance Examination)

| Requirement | Completed Gaokao | Gaokao after application closing date |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum to apply | Gaokao results | Senior Middle 2 (高二) Semester 2 results |
| Academic standard | A good pass in Gaokao | A good pass in Gaokao by June 2026 |
| Standardised test (AP/ACT/SAT) | See notes below | See notes below |
| English requirement | **Required** (see English Test Scores section) | **Required** |
| Programme eligibility | All programmes | Not eligible for Dentistry/Law/Medicine/Nursing |

Notes (from NUS official page):
- Applicants sitting Gaokao in 2026 must update and submit actual results via the Applicant Portal within
  **3 calendar days** of results release.
- An internet print-out of Gaokao results is provisionally accepted; it must clearly show the applicant's full name.
- Applicants who have taken at least 3 Aptitude Scholastic Test (AST) subjects (including English and
  Mathematics) may provide their scores for consideration, if available.
- Senior Middle 3 students NOT sitting Gaokao as their graduation examination should apply under
  "Other High School Qualifications" with standardised test scores plus latest high school results.

## Route 2: International A-Level (Cambridge CAIE, Edexcel, AQA, OCR, Oxford International AQA, WJEC, London)

| Requirement | Completed A-Level | Completing A-Level after closing date |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum to apply | Advanced Level exam results | AS-Level exam results |
| Academic standard | Good pass in at least 3 A-Level subjects (many applicants submit 4+) | Expected good pass in at least 3 A-Level subjects by end July 2026 |
| Standardised test (AP/ACT/SAT) | Not required | **Required** |
| English requirement | Not required | Not required |
| Programme eligibility | All programmes | Not eligible for Dentistry/Law/Medicine |

Notes:
- A-Level subject grades (excluding AS-Level) accepted if obtained in **up to 2 examination sessions**,
  from **up to 2 different examination boards**, all within a **12-month period**.
- For Edexcel International A-Level, the A-Level grade refers to the final grade received upon cash-in.
- Assessment focuses on **content-based subjects** (e.g. Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Economics,
  Accounting, Business).
- Cambridge AICE Diploma holders may also apply.
- English test scores (IELTS, TOEFL, C1 Advanced, EL1119, MUET) may be submitted but are not an
  admission requirement for this route.
- March 2026 A-Level session / 2026 AP test takers: submit score reports via Applicant Portal within
  3 calendar days of results release.

## Route 3: American High School Diploma (AP/SAT/ACT route)

| Requirement | Completed high school | Completing after closing date |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum to apply | American High School Diploma + transcript | Most recent high school exam results before graduation |
| Academic standard | A good pass certificate | Expected good pass certificate by end July 2026 |
| Standardised test (AP/ACT/SAT) | **Required** | **Required** |
| English requirement | Not required | Not required |
| Programme eligibility | All programmes | Not eligible for Dentistry/Law/Medicine |

- Must submit latest high school results, an explanation of the school's grading system, and standardised tests.
- 2026 AP test takers: submit AP scores via Applicant Portal within 3 calendar days of results release.

### Standardised test minimums (official NUS document, Dec 2025)

Accepted combinations: **at least 5 AP courses**, OR **ACT with Writing + at least 3 AP courses**,
OR **SAT + at least 3 AP courses**.

- **AP**: score of **3 in each AP course**; **AP Calculus BC is compulsory for all programmes except Law**
  (Law applicants may use AP Calculus AB). AP results from up to two sittings accepted. Official scores must
  be sent via College Board to NUS (Institute Code **3720**) and uploaded to the Applicant Portal.
- **ACT with Writing**: Composite **29**, Writing **8**; all sections in one sitting; valid 5 years;
  send official scores to NUS (Institute Code **5286**).
- **SAT**: Reading & Writing **600**, Mathematics **650**; all sections in one sitting; valid 5 years;
  send via College Board (Institute Code 3720).
- Acceptable AP courses include: Calculus BC, Statistics, Computer Science A, Biology, Chemistry,
  Physics 1/2/C, Macro/Microeconomics, Psychology, English Language/Literature, World/US/European History,
  Human Geography, Chinese Language and Culture, and other listed languages/arts.

## Route 4: IB Diploma

- Applicants of any nationality may apply with the IB Diploma; IB Career-related Programme (CP) graduates
  may also apply. **English must be one of the subjects taken at the IB examination.**
- Mandatory: authorise IB to release transcripts to "National University of Singapore"
  (IB Institute Code **000690**).
- **November 2025 (or earlier) session**: apply with actual results; key in IB Personal Code; outcomes
  progressively by May 2026. Submit English test scores if at least 3 IB subjects were taught in a
  non-English language.
- **May 2026 session**: apply with school-attested predicted results; update IB Personal Code via Applicant
  Portal by **17 June 2026**; ensure NUS receives the IB transcript by **7 July 2026**; outcomes by the
  third week of July 2026.
- Dentistry applicants: submit predicted IB results + SAT/ACT with Writing + AP exams by 23 February 2026.
- Medicine applicants: predicted IB results + either UCAT (UK or ANZ) or (SAT/ACT with Writing + AP exams)
  by 23 February 2026.
- Mother Tongue Language (MTL) requirement: applies to Singapore Citizens/PRs and to international students
  from specific Singapore independent schools; for international students from IB World Schools outside
  Singapore, the MTL requirement is **not applicable**.

## Route 5: Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level

This route mainly serves students who sat the Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level (e.g. in Singapore JCs).
Application window for AY2026/2027: **26 February 2026 (10am SGT) to 19 March 2026**. The detailed
admission-requirements page was not retrievable at verification time (bot-protection block) — see source:
https://www.nus.edu.sg/oam/admissions/singapore-cambridge-gce-a-level/admission-requirements

## English Language Requirement (applies to Gaokao route and others where flagged)

Minimum acceptable scores (official NUS document, Nov 2025):

| Test | Minimum score | Validity |
|---|---|---|
| IELTS Academic | **6.5 overall, 6.5 in Reading and Writing** | 2 years |
| TOEFL iBT (incl. Home/Paper Edition) | **92–93** | 2 years |
| TOEFL Essentials | 10 | 2 years |
| PTE Academic / PTE Academic Online | 62 overall, Reading and Writing | 2 years |
| C1 Advanced (Cambridge) | 180 | 2 years |
| EL1119 | C6 | — |
| MUET | 240 (2021 onwards) | 5 years |

- IELTS Indicator and IELTS One Skill Retake are **not** accepted.
- Only TOEFL scores from a single test date accepted (no MyBest scores).
- Scores must be within validity period at the application deadline.

## Key practical points for Chinese applicants

- Gaokao route **requires** an English test (IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 92-93); A-Level, AP and IB routes generally do not
  (IB only if 3+ subjects taught in non-English).
- Applying before final exams (Gaokao/A-Level/AP in the application year) excludes Dentistry/Law/Medicine
  (and Nursing for Gaokao route).
- All international-qualification applicants must also meet programme subject prerequisites
  (see nus-programme-prerequisites).
