# NUS Tuition Fees and the MOE Tuition Grant (International Students)

## MOE Tuition Grant — how it works

- The **MOE Tuition Grant (TG)** is a Singapore Government subsidy for full-time undergraduates at
  autonomous universities. Singapore Citizens receive it automatically; **PRs and international students
  must apply** for it.
- Students who accept the TG pay the **subsidised** tuition fee tier; those who decline pay
  **non-subsidised (full) fees** throughout their studies.
- **Service obligation (bond)**: PRs and international students who take the TG must **work for a
  Singapore-registered entity for 3 years after graduation**. Singapore Citizens have no bond
  (except PSC scholars).
- Medicine/Dentistry: all students sign a bond with the Ministry of Health regardless of TG —
  Medicine: 5 years (SC) / 6 years (PR & international); Dentistry: 4 years (SC) / 5 years (PR & international).
- TG application and signing is done via the MOE Tuition Grant and Scholarships portal (tgs.moe.gov.sg)
  in September (Sem 1 intake) or February (Sem 2). Missing the deadline means being charged
  non-subsidised fees.

S$18,050–21,150.

## Key fee policies

- **Cohort-based fees**: tuition fee for an intake cohort is fixed for the whole candidature
  (since AY2008/09). Fees are published annually around Feb–Apr.
- **Miscellaneous student fees** are charged on top of tuition each semester (see official MSF document
  on the Registrar fees page for amounts).
- **Beyond normal candidature**: students taking longer than the normal candidature pay partial-to-full
  non-subsidised fees (capped at 120% of subsidised semestral fees in the 1st extended semester, 150% in
  the 2nd, full pro-rated fees from the 3rd).
- **Refunds**: leaving after Instructional Week 2 of a semester means liability for the full semester's fees.

## Scholarships for international freshmen (NUS Office of Admissions list)

Official scholarships listed for freshmen who are international students:

- **NUS International Undergraduate Scholarship**
- **Science & Technology Undergraduate Scholarship**
- **ASEAN Undergraduate Scholarship** (ASEAN nationals — not applicable to PRC nationals)
- **The Association of Banks in Singapore – Goh Keng Swee Scholarship**
- Additional scholarships administered by NUS faculties/offices and external organisations (see
  https://www.nus.edu.sg/oam/scholarships for terms; award quanta not stated on the listing page — see source).

Scholarship application dates and process: https://www.nus.edu.sg/oam/scholarships/important-dates-and-application

## Financial aid

- Financial aid (loans, bursaries, work-study) is administered via the NUS Office of Admissions /
  Office of Financial Aid; eligibility varies and most schemes target Singapore Citizens/PRs.
  See https://www.nus.edu.sg/oam/financial-aid for the full scheme list and the Financial Aid Calculator.
- Living costs guidance: https://www.nus.edu.sg/oam/financial-aid/living-costs

## Practical summary for Chinese students

- Expect roughly **S$21,000–23,000/year with the MOE Tuition Grant** (plus a 3-year Singapore work bond)
  or roughly **S$33,000–40,000/year without it** for Computing, Business, Engineering and Humanities &
  Sciences programmes (AY2026/2027 cohort; GST included).
- The TG bond requires working for a Singapore entity for 3 years after graduation — a major decision
  point worth flagging to applicants and parents.
