# NTU Key Dates — Admissions to Academic Year 2026/27

NTU has **one intake per academic year**; term typically commences in **late July**. Applicants apply under one of five groups matching their high school qualification, regardless of nationality.

## Document and result deadlines

- **Supporting documents:** upload per online Checklist within **3 days after the application closing date**.
- **Actual results (for applicants awaiting results):** key in and upload within **3 days of release**; otherwise the application is deemed incomplete. No provisional offers on predicted grades (IB, A-Level and several other groups).
- **Final results cut-off:** must be available by **31 July** of the application year (by **31 March** for LKCMedicine and Renaissance Engineering; by mid-July for Art, Design & Media). Results after that ⇒ apply next academic year.
- **AP scores** (American HS route): must reach NTU by **15 July 2026**.
- **English test scores** (IELTS/TOEFL/PTE/SAT/ACT/AST): must be submitted by the deadline for each qualification; **no late submission**. IELTS/TOEFL/PTE valid 2 years; SAT/ACT valid 5 years.
- **Application fee:** SGD 25 (international) / SGD 15 (SC/SPR); non-payment by closing deadline ⇒ application not processed.
- **LKCMedicine:** shortlisted applicants attend Multiple Mini Interviews (MMI) in **April**; no invitation by early April means the Medicine application was unsuccessful.
- **ADM (Art, Design & Media):** portfolio must be uploaded by the application closing date.

## Notes for Chinese (Gaokao) applicants

- The Gaokao group closes **20 Jan 2026** — about two months earlier than the IB/A-Level/American HS groups. Students planning to take the June 2026 Gaokao apply with Gao 1/Gao 2 results before this deadline.
- From AY2027: interview eligibility will be based on **actual Gaokao results** (submitted within 3 days of release), with interviews held in July.
- Scholarship applications (e.g. Nanyang Scholarship) are submitted online within the application period via the Check Status portal.
