# Typical Ages and Timelines per Pathway

Ages below are the **typical** Singapore cohort ages (a child turns the stated age during that school year). International entrants are often 1–2 years older than the local cohort, especially if they repeat a level after AEIS or spend time on English preparation. The Singapore school year runs **January–November/December**.

## 1. Baseline: the local timeline (official structure)

| Stage | Level | Typical age | Exit exam |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | P1–P6 (6 yrs) | 7–12 | PSLE at ~12 |
| Secondary | Sec 1–4/5 (4–5 yrs) | 13–16/17 | O-Level at ~16 (Sec 4 Express) |
| Junior College | JC1–JC2 (2 yrs) | 17–18 | Singapore-Cambridge A-Level at ~18 |
| Millennia Institute | 3 yrs | 17–19 | A-Level at ~19 |
| Polytechnic | Year 1–3 (3 yrs) | 17–19 | Diploma at ~19 |
| University | matriculates Aug | ~19 (women/foreigners) | — |

## 2. AEIS entry points (official facts)

- **AEIS** admits into **Primary 2–5 and Secondary 1–3** only; applications ~July, tests ~September–October, school start the following **January** (moe.gov.sg, verified Jun 2026).
- **S-AEIS** admits into **Primary 2–4 and Secondary 1–2** for the same year (applications ~Jan–Feb, start ~mid-year).
- No direct AEIS entry to P6, Sec 4, or JC — an international student must enter by Sec 3 at the latest (typical age 15) to reach the O-Level through MOE schools.

**Guidance:** MOE may place AEIS entrants one level below their age cohort, so plan for the possibility of being a year "behind".

## 3. Route timelines to NUS/NTU matriculation

### Route A — AEIS → O-Level → JC → A-Level (mainstream)
- Enter MOE secondary via AEIS (Sec 1–3, ages ~13–15).
- **O-Level at ~16** (end of Sec 4) → JAE posting in January.
- **JC: 2 years** (ages 17–18) → **A-Level exams Oct–Dec of JC2**, results released ~February.
- Apply to NUS/NTU **Feb–Mar**, outcomes by **~May**, **matriculate August** of the same year (university Academic Year starts August).
- Total from Sec 1: ~6 years; typical matriculation age **~19** (often 20–21 for late AEIS entrants).

### Route B — O-Level → Polytechnic → NUS/NTU
- **Polytechnic: 3 years** after O-Level (ages ~17–19), graduate ~April–May.
- Apply in the same cycle (Jan–Mar of final poly year, using interim GPA) → matriculate August.
- One year longer than the JC route; typical matriculation age **~20**.

### Route C — IB Diploma
- **IB DP: 2 years** (equivalent to JC years, ages 17–18).
- **November exam session** (most Singapore schools, e.g. ACS(I), SJI): results early January → apply Feb–Mar → matriculate August (~19).
- **May exam session** (most international schools, e.g. UWCSEA): results early July → NUS/NTU outcomes by ~third week of July → matriculate that August (tight) — applications are submitted earlier using predicted grades (official: NUS OAM release-of-outcome table).

### Route D — NUS High School
- 6-year programme (ages ~13–18), Diploma awarded end of Year 6 → apply Feb–Mar → matriculate August (~19).

### Route E — apply from China (Gaokao / international qualifications)
- Gaokao in June of Year 12; NUS/NTU applications for that August's intake generally close earlier (~Feb–Mar), so most Gaokao applicants apply with predicted/previous-year results or wait a year. Outcomes by ~July. Matriculation age ~19.

## 4. National Service note (official)

- **Male Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents** must complete ~2 years of full-time National Service after JC/poly before university; universities reserve places for returning NSmen. This is why local male students matriculate at ~21.
- **National Service does NOT apply to foreigners** on student passes. International male students matriculate straight after A-Level/poly/IB — N/A for the typical Novara user (note: taking up PR triggers NS liability for males).

## 5. Annual cycle cheat-sheet (guidance)

| Month | What happens |
|---|---|
| Jan | School year starts; JAE posting; S-AEIS applications |
| Feb–Mar | A-Level results; NUS/NTU application window closes |
| Apr–May | Local-group admission outcomes released |
| Jul | AEIS applications open; international-qualification outcomes |
| Aug | University Academic Year begins (matriculation) |
| Sep–Oct | AEIS tests |
| Oct–Dec | A-Level / Nov IB exams |

Always confirm the current year's dates on moe.gov.sg, nus.edu.sg/oam/admissions/important-dates and ntu.edu.sg before advising on deadlines.
